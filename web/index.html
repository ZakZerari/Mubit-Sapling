Findings

Every checked line, scored by how strongly it leans toward violating its clause — not just a pass/fail label.

    Why a lean score, not just a verdict: Sapling is calibrated with conformal prediction, and on most of these lines it reports status unsure — meaning insufficient_information stays on the table alongside the real answer, exactly as its model card warns can happen outside clean in-distribution data. But the violation vs. supported contrast underneath still separates clearly: here, the one clause actually breached scores roughly 80% toward violation, while the three clauses actually followed score 68–84% toward supported. The lean score is that contrast, isolated — violation ÷ (violation + supported) — shown alongside the full calibrated output, never instead of it.

    Model: Banyan Sapling by MubitLabs, research preview. Retrieval here is a simple clause-ID match (regex over CL-<number> tokens) — not the point of the demo, just enough to pair each line with its clause.
